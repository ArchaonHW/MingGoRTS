## Story Foundation / 故事基礎

### Narrative Premise / 敘事前提

**English:**
In China's turbulent Republican era (1912-1949), you are a young military officer who rose from poverty, torn between loyalty to your warlord benefactor and your ideals of national unity. Command troops through historical conflicts—from the Northern Expedition to the Anti-Japanese War and Civil War—where every tactical victory advances your career, but every political alliance forces moral compromises. Navigate warlord politics, foreign imperialism, and ideological revolution while witnessing China's painful transformation from empire to republic. Can you forge a path that unifies the nation while staying true to your principles? Or will the pursuit of power and survival corrupt your soul? The fate of millions rests on your choices between loyalty, idealism, pragmatism, and survival.

**繁體中文:**
在中國動盪的民國時期（1912-1949），你是一名從貧困中崛起的年輕軍事軍官，在對軍閥恩人的忠誠和國家統一的理想之間掙扎。指揮部隊穿越歷史衝突——從北伐到抗日戰爭和內戰——在那裡每次戰術勝利都推進你的事業，但每次政治聯盟都迫使你做出道德妥協。在軍閥政治、外國帝國主義和意識形態革命中航行，同時見證中國從帝國到共和國的痛苦轉型。你能開闢一條統一國家同時堅持原則的道路嗎？還是對權力和生存的追求會腐蝕你的靈魂？數百萬人的命運取決於你在忠誠、理想、實用主義和生存之間的選擇。

## Pacing and Flow / 節奏與流動

### Narrative Tempo / 敘事節奏

**Slow burn with intense bursts / 緩慢燃燒，激烈爆發**
- Extended strategic phases between major battles / 大戰之間的延伸策略階段
- Sudden intense narrative moments during campaigns / 戰役期間突然的激烈敘事時刻

### Tension Curve / 張力曲線

**Escalating waves: / 遞增波浪：**
Warlord chaos → National unification → Foreign invasion → Civil war / 軍閥混亂→國家統一→外國入侵→內戰

### Story Density / 故事密度

- **Heavy / 密集:** Campaign missions (linear story) / 戰役任務（線性故事）
- **Light / 輕鬆:** Sandbox mode (player-driven) / 沙盒模式（玩家驅動）

### Key Moments / 關鍵時刻

**Highest tension / 最高張力:** The Invasion (1937) - existential threat / 入侵(1937) - 生存威脅
**Emotional climax / 情感高潮:** The Choice (1949) - final loyalty test / 抉擇(1949) - 最終忠誠考驗
**Resolution beat / 結局節拍:** Player's chosen ending / 玩家選擇的結局

**Methods / 手段:** Guerrilla warfare, political mobilization, propaganda / 游擊戰、政治動員、宣傳

## Character Arcs / 角色弧光

### Protagonist Arc / 主角弧光

**Arc Type / 弧光類型:** Transformation (based on player choices) / 轉變（基於玩家選擇）

**Starting State / 起始狀態:**
- Young, ambitious, seeking opportunity / 年輕、有野心、尋找機會
- Loyal to benefactor, idealistic about China's future / 對恩人忠誠、對中國未來理想化

**Transformation Moments / 轉變時刻:**
1. Mentor's death - loss of innocence / 導師死亡 - 失去純真
2. Political purge - first moral compromise / 政治清洗 - 首次道德妥協
3. Japanese invasion - national identity crisis / 日寇入侵 - 國家認同危機
4. Civil war climax - final loyalty test / 內戰高潮 - 最終忠誠考驗

**Possible Ending States / 可能的結局狀態:**
- **Idealist Ending / 理想主義結局:** Unified China under democratic principles / 民主原則下的統一中國
- **Pragmatist Ending / 實用主義結局:** Powerful but compromised leader / 強大但妥協的領導者
- **Tragic Ending / 悲劇結局:** Destroyed by the very power sought / 被追求的權力本身毀滅
- **Exile Ending / 流亡結局:** Survived but fled to Taiwan or overseas / 倖存但逃往台灣或海外

**Lessons Learned / 學到的教訓:**
- Power corrupts without moral anchors / 沒有道德錨點，權力會腐化

### Factions and Organizations / 派系與組織

**Leadership / 領導人:** Sun Yat-sen (孫中山, 1866-1925) → Chiang Kai-shek (蔣介石)

**Key Moments / 關鍵時刻：**

- **Democracy / 民權主義:** Establish republican government / 建立共和政府

## Dialogue Framework / 對話框架

### Dialogue Style / 對話風格

**Overall Voice / 整體語調:**
- **Formality / 正式度:** Formal military and political speech / 正式的軍事和政治語言
- **Period / 時代感:** Period-appropriate (1920s-1940s Chinese) / 符合時代（1920-40年代中文）
- **Verbosity / 詳盡度:** Moderate - military brevity mixed with political rhetoric / 適中 - 軍事簡潔混合政治修辭
- **Humor / 幽默:** Serious with occasional dry wit / 嚴肅帶偶爾冷幽默
- **Profanity / 粗俗語言:** Mild period-appropriate exclamations / 輕度符合時代的感歎

**Character Voice Distinctions / 角色語言區別:**
| Character Type / 角色類型 | Speech Style / 語言風格 |
|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Officers / 軍官 | Formal, hierarchical, mission-focused / 正式、階級化、任務導向 |
