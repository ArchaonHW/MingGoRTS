# Step 4: Target Audience

**Goal:** Clearly define the target audience for this product and create detailed user personas.

## Your Task:

Help me understand our players. Let's brainstorm some user personas. For example:
*   **"Hardcore Henry"**: A veteran of strategy games. What does he look for?
*   **"Curious Chloe"**: A history enthusiast new to RTS games. How can we make the game accessible to her?
*   **"Social Sam"**: A player who enjoys multiplayer and community aspects.

Let's define their goals, motivations, and potential frustrations.
