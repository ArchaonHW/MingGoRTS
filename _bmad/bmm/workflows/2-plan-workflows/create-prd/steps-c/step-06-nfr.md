# Step 6: Non-Functional Requirements

**Goal:** Define the system's technical requirements, such as performance, scalability, and security.

## Your Task:

Let's consider the technical aspects. For example:
*   **Performance:** What is our target framerate (e.g., 60fps)? How many units can be on screen at once?
*   **Scalability:** How will the game handle large-scale multiplayer battles?
*   **Platform:** What are our target specs for PC, and our target devices for mobile?

Your input here will be crucial for the architects and developers.
