# Step 3: Executive Summary

**Goal:** Write a concise, high-level summary of the project's vision, goals, and core value proposition. This should be understandable by a non-technical stakeholder in 30 seconds.

## Your Task:

Based on the Product Vision we've defined, draft a 1-2 paragraph Executive Summary. It should briefly answer:
1.  What is this product?
2.  Who is it for?
3.  Why is it valuable?

Once you provide the draft, I will append it to the `prd.md` document.
